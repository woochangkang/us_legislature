Caucuses

American Seafood Caucus (Co-Chair)
The American Seafood Caucus brings together Members from coastal states where the domestic seafood industry is critical to the state and local economy. The caucus focuses on issues that grow the domestic industry and promote domestically sourced seafood.

Chesapeake Bay Watershed Task Force (Co-Chair)
The Chesapeake Bay Watershed Task Force brings together multiple partners to chart a path forward in Congress for the Chesapeake Bay. As co-chair of the Chesapeake Bay Watershed Caucus, my priorities include restoring the Bay by addressing issues pertaining to conservation, funding, restoration, and raising awareness of these issues to other Members of Congress.

Congressional Protein Innovation Caucus (Co-Chair)
The Protein Innovation Caucus is a bipartisan coalition of Members who are committed to ensuring America leads innovation in the protein sector of the larger biotech industry in order to ensure greater food security, increase American competitiveness and safeguard our economic interests. This caucus will serve as a platform for education, inspiration, and coordination related to the innovative protein sector and its related policy priorities.

Congressional Public Health Caucus (Co-Chair)
As co-chair of the Congressional Public Health Caucus, my shared priority is to support federal policies that promote awareness and prevention to improve the overall health of the American people. This Caucus provides a platform for Members of Congress to learn about public health issues, such as food safety and pandemics, and to discuss development solutions that will make the American public healthier and reduce healthcare costs.

Congressional Quad Caucus (Co-Chair)
Cooperation between the United States, Japan, India, and Australia is crucial for the future stability of the Indo-Pacific. The Quad’s support for governance of emerging technologies, countering illegal fishing, and enhanced maritime domain awareness proves that we will build a better future for the region by working together. I’m proud to serve as the co-chair of the bicameral, bipartisan Quad Caucus to foster stable collaboration for years to come.

Congressional Rural Broadband Caucus (Co-Chair)
As co-chair of the Congressional Rural Broadband Caucus, I’m working with fellow Members to ensure our nation’s rural and unserved populations have the telecommunication resources to participate in a 21st century digital world. This bipartisan caucus aims to educate and facilitate discussions amongst Members of Congress to close the digital divide between urban and rural communities across the nation.

Congressional Shellfish Caucus (Co-Chair)
The Shellfish Caucus is a bipartisan group of Members of Congress working to enhance the understanding and awareness of the issues and policies that affect the United States’ shellfish industry including its harvesters, processors, distributors and consumers

Congressional Shipbuilding Caucus (Co-Chair)
As a co-chair of the Congressional Shipbuilding Caucus, I share three main objectives: to build a venue for Members to discuss shipbuilding issues that impact their districts, to work together with other Members to support increased shipbuilding efforts for the United States Navy that will enhance our national security, and to improve awareness regarding shipbuilding matters across Capitol Hill. Our caucus provides a forum for Members of Congress, Navy, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard officers, and industry leaders to exchange views on the current and future states of domestic shipbuilding.

Congressional Submarine Caucus (Co-Chair)
Submarines are built in only two states, and one of them is Virginia. As co-chair of the Congressional Submarine Caucus, my goal is to advocate for our naval warfighters and shipbuilders to strengthen the undersea advantage our Navy holds over our adversaries. No other country has more nuclear-powered submarines, nor can they compete with the technological edge of our Virginia-built submarines. Supporting our submarine force supports Virginia and supports the defense of our greatest military advantage.

Congressional Suburban Caucus (Co-Chair)
As co-chair of the Congressional Suburban Caucus, I’m working to formulate policies that positively affect the everyday lives of suburban America. By working with my fellow members who are invested in the success and growth of suburban America, the Caucus will bring together the best ideas of its members into an agenda for the future.

Congressional Toy Caucus (Co-Chair)
The Toy Caucus is dedicated to raising awareness on issues impacting the toy industry, including ensuring product safety; protecting children's online safety; securing resilient supply chains; mitigating the sale of illegal and counterfeit products; and advancing environmental sustainability.

Congressional Wildlife Refuges Caucus (Co-Chair)
Our nation’s wildlife refuges are incredibly important in protecting America’s fish, wildlife, and plants. As co-chair of the Congressional Wildlife Refuge Caucus, my priority is to preserve the National Wildlife Refuge System by raising awareness of our nation’s refuges among Members of Congress and promote legislation to improve the Refuge system.

Defense Modernization Caucus (Co-Chair)
As a founding co-chair of the Defense Modernization Caucus, I work to amplify and champion kep policy and funding priorities necessary for the technological evolution of the U.S. Department of Defense and other kep national security functions. As the nature of warfare evolves, Congress must change its focus to support flexible and responsive modernization efforts that rapidly integrate emerging technologies that will help our warfighters win.

Public Shipyard Caucus (Co-Chair)
I’m proud to serve as co-chair of the Public Shipyard Caucus to support our nation’s four public shipyards – Norfolk Naval Shipyard in Virginia, Portsmouth Naval Shipyard in Maine, Puget Sound Naval Shipyard and Intermediate Maintenance Facility in Washington, and Pearl Harbor Naval Shipyard Intermediate Maintenance Facility in Hawaii. Our Caucus serves as a venue to find solutions to public shipyard issues, increase stakeholder support for public shipyards, and raise awareness of their importance to Congress.
